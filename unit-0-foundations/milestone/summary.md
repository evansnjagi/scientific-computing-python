# Sensor Reading Summary
> A PM2.5 value is a concentration of microscopic particulate matter. It's measured in micro grams per cubic meter. 

## Important 
between 0 - 9 $g|m^3$ - Good air

10 - 35 - Moderate air

36 - 55 - Unhealthy air for children and elderly

56+ - Hazardeous to everyone

## Sensor Analysis Script
The script involves writting proper functions that:

1. computes average
2. computes standard deviation
3. Returns readings that are above and below average; readings that are above or below one standard deviation.
4. Compute the maximum sensor reading as well as the minimum.
