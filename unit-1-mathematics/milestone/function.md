Choice function:
$$
    f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 + 4x - 6y
$$

Critical point is:
$$
    \frac{df}{dx} = 2x + 4 \\
    2x + 4 = 0\\
    2x = -4\\
    x = -2
$$
$$
\frac{df}{dy} = 2y - 6 \\
2y - 6 = 0\\
2y = 6\\
y = 3  
$$
The critical point is $(-2, 3)$

Therefore, the minimal point is the solution to the function `f(x, y)` using the critical point (-2, 3).
$$
    (-2)^2 + (3)^2 + 4(-2) - 6(3)\\
    4 + 9 - 8 - 18\\
    \implies -13
$$
