# Differentiation Rules

## 1. power rule

$$
    \frac{d}{dx} x^n = nx^{n -1}
$$

## 2. Constant rule

$$
    \frac{d}{dx}c = 0
$$

## 3. Sum rule

$$
    \frac{d}{dx}[f(x) + g(x)] = f'(x) + g'(x)
$$

## 4. Chain rule
This is the most import rule in ML

$$
    \frac{d}{dx} g(h(x)) = g'(h(x)) \cdot h'(x)
$$
Differentiate outer, keep inner constant, then multiply by differentiation of inner.
